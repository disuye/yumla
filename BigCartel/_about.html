Lower Basement, 79 Wyndham Street, Central, Hong Kong.

Welcome to...

1999 ~ 2002 – Phi-b
2003 ~ 2012 – Yumla
2013 ~ 2015 – Midnight & Co.
2015 ~ present – Oma

Same basement, different times, different venues, different people, similar hopes and dreams. That tiny basement fast is approaching 30 non-stop years of tunes, booze, people. Probably the best tunes and certainly the best people. The booze was always questionable.

What began life as a sealed void in the foundations of a commercial building; later made accessible via internal stairs and used for storage; then – with rising property prices – converted into a rental unit with it's very own door connecting to the outside world; an important sub-culture space and part of the history and fabric of Hong Kong.
